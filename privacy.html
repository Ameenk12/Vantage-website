Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Welcome to Vantage. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, and authentication data (such as via Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In). As a fitness app, we also store data you actively provide, such as workout logs, nutritional entries, and body metrics.

2. Local-First Architecture

Vantage is built with a local-first philosophy. The majority of your data processing happens directly on your device. Cloud sync is provided as a convenience to back up your data and sync across devices, but you maintain ownership and can export your data at any time.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide the core functionality of the Vantage app, including syncing your data across devices, personalizing your experience, and offering customer support. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Vantage's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your account information and app data are securely stored using encrypted cloud services powered by Supabase.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@vantage.support.
