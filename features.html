Frictionless Nutrition

Typing out every ingredient is tedious. With Vantage, you can just tap the microphone and speak your meal naturally. Our AI extracts the foods, matches them against the USDA database, and logs the macros instantly.

  • Voice dictation powered by OpenAI Whisper
  • Barcode scanner for packaged foods
  • Recipe importer via iOS Share Sheet
  • Adaptive calorie targets that adjust to your true burn
