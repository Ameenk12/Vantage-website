What’s in the current build

A serious log that stays out of your way.

Vantage handles the repetitive parts of training and nutrition, then puts the useful patterns in one place.

NutritionTrainingProgressDevicesNext

01

Nutrition

Describe the meal. Review the result.

Speak or type naturally, search USDA and manual foods, scan a packaged food, reuse a saved meal, or create a recipe from a link, image, paste, or conversation.

Daily diary
Calories and macros by meal, with seven-day context.
Flexible targets
Fixed goals or adaptive guidance based on body data you choose to track.
Review first
Estimated food stays reviewable before it enters your record.

02

Training

Programs when you want structure. Quick start when you don’t.

Run a program, reuse a template, or start from an empty session. Previous performance, set targets, exercise notes, plate math, rest timing, and workout tools stay close.

890 exercises
Search by equipment, movement, and muscle group, with instructions and body maps.
More than reps
Track weight, duration, distance, warm-ups, and working sets with the right units.
Local first
The logger continues working in a gym with unreliable reception.

03

Progress

Compare the same period across the whole routine.

See workouts, volume, time trained, calorie intake, body weight, exercise performance, and personal records over consistent ranges.

Period comparisons
Seven, 30, 90, all-time, and custom ranges.
Training detail
Strength estimates, history, records, and muscle-group balance.
One share action
Export a readable summary without hunting through separate sections.

Across your devices

The same record, fitted to the screen.

iPhone

The complete day-to-day app with native navigation and focused one-column flows.

iPad

Adaptive dashboards, wider charts, denser grids, and a live-workout sidebar.

Apple Watch

Start workouts, log sets, follow rest, view the diary, use saved meals, and dictate food.
