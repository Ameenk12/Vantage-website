Nutrition

Log the meal the way it happened.

Describe a meal by voice or text, scan a packaged food, repeat a saved meal, or create a recipe from a photo, link, paste, or chat.

  • Natural meal loggingSpeak or type in your own words, review the estimate, then decide what to save.
  • A real daily diaryCalories, protein, carbs, fat, meal groups, and seven-day context stay readable.
  • Flexible targetsUse fixed goals or adaptive calorie guidance based on the body data you choose to track.

Training

Everything the set needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

Start from a program, template, or quick workout. Previous performance, set targets, notes, plate math, rest timing, and exercise detail remain close.

  • 890-exercise librarySearch across equipment, movement patterns, and muscle groups with instructions and body maps.
  • Every kind of setTrack weight and reps, duration, or distance with the correct units.
  • Local-first sessionsThe active workout remains usable in a gym with unreliable reception.

Progress

Useful patterns, not vanity metrics.

Compare time ranges across workouts, volume, time trained, calorie intake, body weight, and individual exercise performance.

  • At-a-glance summariesPeriod-over-period context explains what changed instead of leaving you with a chart alone.
  • Strength detailFollow estimated strength, personal records, history, and muscle-group balance.
  • Sherpa coachingAsk for practical help grounded in the Vantage data you choose to share with the conversation.
