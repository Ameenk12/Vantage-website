Product principles

Keep the record useful. Keep the person in control.

Vantage puts training and nutrition in one practical system without requiring a social profile, an always-on connection, or blind trust in an estimate.

Local first, by design

A workout or meal should not depend on a perfect connection. Core records are written on your device first, and an account is optional. Sign in when you want cloud backup, sync, or provider-backed features.

Your data stays useful to you

Vantage does not need a social feed or advertising profile to work. You can review and edit your entries, export supported data, clear local records, or delete an account from the app.

AI earns its place

Provider-backed tools are used for jobs where they reduce real friction: transcribing speech, turning a natural meal description into a reviewable estimate, importing recipes, generating plans, and helping Sherpa respond to the context you choose to provide. Those features require an account and are separate from local workout and meal logging.

One system, two modes

Nutrition is calm and spacious. Training is focused and numeric. They share one design language, one progress story, and the same commitment to clear controls, accessible states, and purposeful motion.

Built in the open with its own evidence

The app is still in private beta. Product claims are tied to implemented behavior, automated checks, and rendered verification. When something remains in testing, Vantage says so.
