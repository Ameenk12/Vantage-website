Our Mission

We believe your health data belongs to you, and your tools should get out of your way.

The Problem

Most fitness apps today are bloated with social feeds, invasive ads, and dark patterns designed to keep you scrolling. Even worse, they hold your data hostage in the cloud, rendering the app useless the moment you step into a gym with poor cell reception.

The Vantage Philosophy

1. Local-First

Vantage is built on a local-first architecture. Your data is saved instantly to your device. This means the app is blazingly fast and works perfectly offline. Cloud sync is a feature for convenience and multi-device support—not a requirement to use the app.

2. Your Data is Yours

We don't sell your data. We don't hold it hostage. You can export your entire database (workouts, nutrition, body metrics, and recipes) to JSON or CSV at any time, straight from the app settings.

3. AI as an Assistant, Not a Gimmick

We use artificial intelligence where it actually solves a problem. We use OpenAI's Whisper for voice dictation so you don't have to type out meals, and Anthropic's Claude for CBT-style habit coaching grounded in your real logged data. We never use AI to fabricate exercise instructions or guess at unverified nutrition facts.
